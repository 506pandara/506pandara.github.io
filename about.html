About Us

Who We Are

The story, mission, and program behind FTC Team 506 Pandara.

Our Mission

Team Pandara exists to inspire students to become confident, creative problem-solvers through the design, engineering, and competition of FIRST Tech Challenge robots — while fostering leadership, mentorship, and a lasting passion for STEM both inside and outside the classroom.

Our Story

Founded in 2007 at Palm Harbor University High School in Palm Harbor, Florida, Team 506 Pandara has grown from a small group of curious students into a competitive FIRST Tech Challenge program with a track record of engineering excellence, community outreach, and success on the world stage.

The Program

What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

A bit of background for anyone new to FTC.

Grades 7–12

FIRST Tech Challenge is an international robotics competition for students in grades 7 through 12, run by FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology).

A New Game Every Year

Each season, teams design, build, and program a robot to compete in a brand-new game revealed at kickoff, then compete head-to-head in alliances at qualifying events, states, and — for top teams — the World Championship.

More Than Robots

Beyond the robot, teams are judged on their engineering notebook, community outreach, and embodiment of FIRST's core values, including Gracious Professionalism and Coopertition.
