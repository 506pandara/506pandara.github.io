About Pandara

Who We Are

Founded in 2007 at Palm Harbor University High School, Team 506 Pandara is a student-led robotics organization competing in the FIRST Tech Challenge. Every season, we design, machine, wire, and program a competition robot from the ground up.

Pandara operates like a small engineering company — dedicated subteams for software, hardware, CAD, and outreach, all working toward the same goal: building a robot, and the leaders behind it.

Road to Worlds

From our first bare-bones robot in 2007 to a state alliance captain and World Championship qualifier, Pandara's growth has been built one season, one robot, and one lesson at a time.

  1. 2007

    Founding Season

    Team 506 Pandara is founded at Palm Harbor University High School, fielding our first FIRST Tech Challenge robot.

  2. Building the Program

    Years of Growth

    Season after season, Pandara built out its engineering process, mentorship pipeline, and competition record across Florida events.

  3. State Championship

    #1 Seed Alliance Captain

    Pandara earned the #1 seed at the Florida FTC State Championship and made the first alliance selection pick of the playoffs.

  4. World Championship

    7th in Division + Think Award

    Pandara qualified for the FIRST Tech Challenge World Championship, finished 7th in our division, and won 1st Place Think Award for our engineering notebook.

  5. Current Season

    Gearing Up for BIOBUZZ

    We're preparing our next robot for BIOBUZZ presented by RTX, the 2026–2027 FIRST Tech Challenge season.
